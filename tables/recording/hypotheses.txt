    SUPPLEMENTARY: NON-INFERIORITY, the inferential version
    H2 above compares POINT ESTIMATES against the tolerance.
    That is what was pre-specified, and the verdict stands on
    it. But a mean inside a margin is not the same claim as
    'demonstrably within the margin', so the test below asks
    the inferential question directly, for each k:

      H0: mean(full - k) >= margin   (the loss is at least
                                      the margin)
      H1: mean(full - k) <  margin   (the loss is smaller)

    margin = 0.01, one-sided, alpha = 0.05.
    The standard error carries the Nadeau-Bengio correction,
    because the paired differences come from repeated k-fold
    and are NOT independent; the interval is therefore wider
    than a naive one, deliberately.

  NOT the pre-registered test; the H3 verdicts above stand on
  the raw Wilcoxon p-values against alpha = 0.05.
  The paired split scores are NOT independent: repeated k-fold
  reuses the same recordings, so training sets overlap and the
  naive variance is too small. This test replaces the 1/n

NOTE ON INDEPENDENCE
  Scores from repeated cross-validation are not fully
  independent: the 2 repeats reuse the same recordings,
  so the paired scores overlap. The p-values above are therefore
  supporting evidence alongside the effect sizes and percentile
  ranks, not standalone proof. Say so in the paper.

NOTE ON SENSOR IMPORTANCE
  Grouped permutation importance measures a sensor's MARGINAL
  contribution. A sensor scoring near zero may carry no
  information, or may be redundant given the other sensors
  present. This method cannot distinguish those two cases; the
  forward-selection order and the sensor correlation matrix are
  reported to help separate them.
